# Human User Guide Template
This is a template to help you create your own human user guide. The goal of a human user guide is to make working together and collaboration with colleagues easier, faster. The focus for this user guide is on the workplace so many of the questions are geared towards information that would be pertinent to your colleagues, but don't be afraid to add your own sparkle! ✨
## About me

### What are some honest, unfiltered things about you?
* 🍖🥘🍨 I love food, cooking, eating, and going to restaurants!! I've had the privilege of eating at several *** Michelin star restaurants but I also make sure to get a $1 keytag every year for free mini frosties. My particular favorites are Korean food and ice cream.
* 🧩 I am a little neuro-picante and tend to go brain > feelings. Sometimes I will have a hard time with idioms / figures of speech.
* 🏁 I have a hard time finishing things. I have a bunch of ideas to start, but I struggle with "completing" something because nothing ever feels quite finished, since I think everything can be improved. I'm working on creating more tangible steps for myself and accepting that just because it can be improved, doesn't mean you have to do it.
	
### What drives you nuts?
* 😖 It's difficult for me to follow conversation when people are evasive or beat around the bush. Please try your best to just tell me what you're trying to say.
* 🙉 It bothers me when people are intensely close-minded. I think there's a lot of nuance in the world and we are all just meat bags trying to make it to the next day, so maybe let your mind tilt a couple of degrees today.

### What are your quirks?
* ♎ I'm a Libra so I really hate conflict lol, but I am down to work through anything to find a solution with others as long as they are willing to work with me. I try to be flexible and adaptable to finding solutions.
* ✍️ I didn't go to a bootcamp or take any CS courses, so a lot of what I've learned about programming is practical and industry-taught. I like to think of good coding as poetry rather than big O notations.
* ⏰ Sometimes I get into a task wormhole and my 5 calendar alerts for a meeting totally go over my head, so if I'm > 3 minutes late to a meeting or call we have, please DM me on Slack and forgive my tardiness and I promise I won't ever be mad if you're ever late to a call we have.
		
### What are some things that people might misunderstand about you that you should clarify?
* 🎊 I can sometimes get really animated when I talk but that's usually because I'm excited about it! I'm almost never really angry about something and if I am I try to let people know as soon as I do.

## About my colleagues

### What qualities do you particularly value in your colleagues?
* 🌬️ I like to work with colleagues that are honest with me. If something is not working or is uncomfortable for you, let me know so that we can just air out all grievances and figure out how to move forward together.
* 🧑‍🍳 I value people who don't think that any kind of work/worker is "beneath" them.
* ☑️ I appreciate coworkers who can give succinct, regular, and informative updates. I don't feel like I need to know what other people are working on, but it makes me feel like a better part of the team when I do.

### What do you wish colleagues didn’t do?
* 🪢 I wish that colleagues didn't avoid owning up to a mistake, not knowing how to do something, or a missed deadline. I would rather us figure it out together than watch you sweat to figure out how to avoid telling me.
* 🆕 I would always try to implement the newest and "coolest" way of doing something, but I think sometimes the best solution isn't the newest one, it's the one that works the best. I wish my colleagues would try to prioritize that and also keep me in check when I'm trying to solve a problem that doesn't exist, just because I want to use the new shiny thing.
	
### How can people earn an extra gold star with you?
* 🥳 I like when people are encouraging or notice when I do something that either made their day a little better or helped them in some way.
* 🎈 I like pairing and solving through problems together - whether technical or design. It also encourages me when we act as if we don't know anything about the problem at hand and try to solve it from 10,000ft and 1mm.

## About my interactions

### How do you coach people to do their best work and develop their talents?
* I like to figure out what motivates people to do their best work. Even though not all work is going to be exciting, I think there's always something to make a problem interesting to a person.
* I prefer collaborative ways of working together and solving problems together. I think this way we can both identify opportunities that we can both excel in or learn from each other.
* I am usually more of a pusher / affirmer than a nurturer, so if you need something a little softer, let me know!
	
### What’s the best way to communicate with you?
1. DM me in Slack, if I don't get back to you just ping me again, it won't bother me. I also don't mind being messaged after-hours but I probably won't respond until the next business day.
2. Impromptu or scheduled Zoom call
3. You can send me an email but no guarantee it'll get read.
	
### What’s the best way to convince you to do something?
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Say that we're going to do it together!
* 🧠 Tell me 3-5 well-reasoned thoughts or strong feelings about what you want (me) to do.
* 🕵️ Give me a problem that has gone unsolved for > 3 months.
* 🎮 Make it into a game.
* 🍘 Bribe me with snacks.
	 
### How do you like to give feedback?
* I like to give feedback immediately after something happens, as I am often nervous about forgetting to give it later. Generally, I like to give feedback informally and through Slack, but if the feedback merits having a longer conversation, I like to Zoom call. I like calls because I think the high-interaction is valuable when talking about potentially sensitive topics.

### How do you like to get feedback?
* I like receiving feedback through any medium, preferably something that is written. It helps me to refer back to things when there is record of it. I also like to receive feedback frequently, since I think it helps me stay more aligned with what my coworkers expect from me.
